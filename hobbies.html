My Hobbies & Interests

One of the best things about being a Student is flexibility with schedules.

When the weather is nice, I love going disc golfing or playing Ultimate Frisbee with my friends.

I love exploring the parks around Duluth, and especially love going rock jumping. I do not, however, enjoy falling in.
