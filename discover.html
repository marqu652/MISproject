Discover Duluth

University of Minnesota, Duluth

Why Duluth?

Duluth has so many amazing things to discover.

Campus Life: Campus life is pretty cool, with a lot of nice spots on campus to hangout or do homework. The classes are usually good, and Duluth has a good Computer Science program.

Food Scene: There's so much good food to find in a city like Duluth. One of my favorite places is Northern Waters Smokehaus.

Nature & Hiking: In Duluth, there is an abundance of nature. One of my favorite spots to hike is Rock Knob (about a 20-minute hike from campus).
