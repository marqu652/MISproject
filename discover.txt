Duluth has so many amazing things to discover.
Campus life is pretty cool, with a lot of nice spots on campus to hangout or do homework.
The classes are usually good, and Duluth has a good Computer Science program.
There's so much good food to find in a city like Duluth. One of my favorite places is Northern Waters Smokehaus.
In Duluth, there is an abundance of nature. One of my favorite spots to hike is Rock Knob (about a 20-minute hike from campus).
